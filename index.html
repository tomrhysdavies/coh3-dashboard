🧠 The Mental Stack

  • Are my resources floating? If you have >500 Manpower, spend it now.
  • Where is the enemy's Fuel? Cutting fuel delays their tanks.
  • Is my retreat path clear? Don't get pinned by snares.
  • Am I using my Munitions? Mines and grenades win games.
