🧠 The Mental Stack

  • Are my resources floating? If you have >500 Manpower, you’re falling behind. Spend it on infantry or global upgrades.
  • Where is the enemy's Fuel? Cutting the enemy's Fuel for even 60 seconds can delay their first tank by several minutes.
  • Is my retreat path clear? Never fight in a spot where a single snare or grenade can block your only way home.
  • Am I using my Munitions? Munitions do zero damage sitting in the bank. Use mines, grenades, and loiters.
